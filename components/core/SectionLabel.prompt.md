**SectionLabel** — the eyebrow divider that opens every page section. A short cyan rule, the label in mono caps, then a hairline fading to the right.

```jsx
<SectionLabel>Services</SectionLabel>
<SectionLabel>Clients I Adore</SectionLabel>
```

Keep labels short and uppercase (the CSS uppercases them anyway). On the live site these "boot" in with a brief `// INITIALIZING...` flash when scrolled into view.
